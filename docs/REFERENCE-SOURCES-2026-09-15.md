# Downloadable Rhodes reference survey

Research date: 2026-09-15. Scope: identifiable recordings of real Rhodes pianos, prioritizing isolated notes, dynamics, releases and direct capture for RF-73. This is a broad candidate survey, not a claim of exhaustive coverage of the internet. Specifications are publisher statements, not measurements of downloaded files. No audio was downloaded, purchases made, accounts created or subscriptions submitted in this survey. A published download route is not a verified completed download.

[Chicago Electric Piano Co / Reverb, Sample Series Vol. 3](https://reverb.com/item/10319985-the-chicago-electric-piano-co-sample-series-vol-3-rhodes-mark-ii) advertises real Mark II multisamples as WAV and an Ableton pack. The inspected listing simultaneously says FREE/Instant Download and shows a Coming Soon button. Keep it as a useful lead, not a confirmed working download. No third-party reupload was selected.

## Playable comparison references and further leads

- [Rhodes Anthology](https://rhodesmusic.com/rhodes-anthology-plugin/): official sampled MK1 Stage 73 (1976), Sparkletop 73 (1965), MKV 73 (1984), MK7 73 (2010). A 14-day trial is advertised. Useful for playing comparisons; not established as downloadable open WAV data.
- [Acousticsamples VTines MK1](https://www.acousticsamples.net/vtines): restored 1978 Mark I, hybrid sampling/modeling, UVI/iLok. Useful comparison instrument; its continuously modeled dynamics must not be mistaken for independent measured layers.
- [Splice INSTRUMENT Vintage Keys](https://splice.com/instrument/packs/vintage-keys): free sampled Seventy Three instrument with four presets. A listening reference; exposed raw samples and capture details were not established.
- [Purgatory Creek Tine Collection](https://www.purgatorycreek.com/index.php/tine-collection-kontakt/): official commercial Kontakt collection located; detailed acquisition/specification check remains pending after a page timeout.
- [SampleSwap Rhodes Electric Piano](https://sampleswap.org/filebrowser-new.php?d=INSTRUMENTS+%28MULTISAMPLED%29%2FSYNTH+KEYS+ETCETERA%2FRhodes+Electric+Piano%2F): free-account WAV directory, but model and capture provenance are insufficient to prioritize physical calibration.
- [keplar's 1977 MK1 recording example](https://freesound.org/s/132063/): direct Focusrite capture and CC BY 4.0, but a short musical phrase rather than a note/layer dataset. Useful only for supplementary musical comparisons.

## Selection and acquisition order

1. Inspect Matt's and Wonder Rhodes for a new Mark I baseline; retain the delivered original files and mapping documents.
2. Expand only the mono jRhodes bank needed for register comparisons, keeping its EQ/denoising limitation explicit.
3. Evaluate ETI ROADS as a separate Mark II set after clarifying instrument-development analysis use. Its thesis/manual are valuable provenance leads even before downloading the large bank.
4. Add tim.kahn, mRhodes and Syrinx Clean for cross-instrument checks after file-level inspection.
5. Consider EP73 or Famous E if the free sets leave specific gaps in repeated strikes or dynamics.

Free access is not an open calibration license. [Pianobook terms](https://www.pianobook.co.uk/terms-conditions/), [ETI terms](https://etiroads.wordpress.com/downloads_en/) and [jRhodes license](https://github.com/sfzinstruments/jlearman.jRhodes3d/blob/master/LICENSE) should accompany the corresponding inventory. No sample redistribution inside RF-73 is proposed.

For every acquired file record source URL/revision, SHA-256, original filename, actual sample rate/bit depth/channels, note pitch, nominal layer, signal path, processing, release/loop boundaries and terms. Never translate layer numbers into measured hammer velocity. Keep gain comparisons within a documented capture chain; use level-matched comparisons separately for timbre. A DI label alone does not establish untreated audio or fixed capture gain.

Do not prioritize processed loop packs, samples of Reface/Nord/software emulators, synthetic Rhodes-like patches, jRhodes stereo duplicates or unverified legacy-library mirrors for physical calibration. No public dataset with measured hammer speeds and pickup geometry was verified in this survey.
